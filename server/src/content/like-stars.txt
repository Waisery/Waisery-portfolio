Why does a glimmer endure?
I have searched for it beyond counting:
in the silence of the deepest night,
before stone tombs where the wind warms old fables,
through a universe of waxing moons and setting suns.

A firefly drifts alone.
Distant blue shadows spread.
That faint, mysterious light
is almost lost in the boundless night.

And yet,
though the first glimmer is weak,
it may still kindle a bright moon;
gathered with others, it longs to illuminate the dark.

Morning sunlight slants across the vast sky.
After the rain, the clear dawn shines in every color.
Small lights blaze,
waking a whole pool of stars.

May
those glimmers travel ten thousand miles,
at last reaching the far shore of the stars;
may they give the world reason to hope
for a light
that dares to rival the sun.

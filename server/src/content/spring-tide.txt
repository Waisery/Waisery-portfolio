Who has endured the endless winds of winter?
Who has wandered through the dark night of pain?
O traveler!
Have you been lost in winter's tide so long
that you have forgotten the lakeside spring you long for?

Yet cross the border into spring:
the ancient woods have put out new branches.
Scattered cherry blossoms sweep through the heart,
and only then do I know:
the hope of the past has overflowed with flowers.

Look for a moment.
This lovely world is already lush with spring.
At this moment, spring has come to life again.
The ancient frozen ice thaws with a splash;
a thread of spring light suddenly appears.
I seem to see the myrtle, after trillions of years,
leading an enchanting song.
